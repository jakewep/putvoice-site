PutVoice Support

Questions, bugs, or ideas — here's how to reach us.

Email support

putvoice@gmail.com

Support is handled by Jake Weppner, the operator of PutVoice.

Send feedback from the app

  1. Open PutVoice and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Send feedback.
  3. Choose Bug, Idea, or Other.
  4. Write what happened or what you'd like to see.
  5. Tap Send via Mail.

The app opens the message in your own Mail app and fills in the PutVoice and iOS versions automatically. It does not include your inventory. If Mail is not set up on your phone, the app shows the support address so you can copy it and write from another account.

What helps us fix a bug

  • What you were trying to do.
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
  • The exact words you dictated, if a voice entry or question was involved.
  • Whether it happens every time or only sometimes.

Please don't send passwords or inventory details you don't want to share by email.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your iPhone. PutVoice has no account system and no server copy. Deleting the app deletes its data, so keep your normal iPhone backups on if your inventory matters to you.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual items in the app, or delete PutVoice from your iPhone to remove all of its on-device data. There is no server copy to request deletion for.

How does privacy work?

Read the PutVoice Privacy Policy for the full detail, including how Apple handles dictation when on-device recognition is unavailable.
