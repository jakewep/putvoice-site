Privacy Policy — PutVoice

Effective date: Effective upon publication in the App Store
App: PutVoice (iOS), bundle ID com.jakewep.personalinventory
Operator: Jake Weppner, reachable at putvoice@gmail.com

The short version

PutVoice stores everything on your iPhone. We don't run a server for the app, there are no accounts, and the app collects nothing — no inventory data, no photos, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, nothing. We couldn't look at your inventory if we wanted to; it is never sent to us.

If you choose to contact support, the app hands you to your own Mail app. We receive only the message you choose to send from your own email account.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your inventory — item names, locations (room, container, spot), quantities, notes, dates, lending records, and history — is stored in a database on your device only.
  • Photos you attach (for example, a photo of the drawer or bin where an item lives) are stored on your device only, in the app's own storage.
  • Settings and preferences are stored on your device only.

None of this is transmitted to us or to any third party. The app works fully offline.

What the app collects

Nothing. Specifically:

  • No accounts or sign-up — there is nothing to register for.
  • No in-app email capture or reply-address field.
  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No crash-reporting or diagnostics services.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data — the app gives us none to sell or share.

Contacting support or sending feedback

The feedback screen opens a message in your own Mail app, addressed to putvoice@gmail.com. PutVoice does not ask for, store, or transmit a reply address. Your Mail app supplies the From address associated with your own email account, and your email provider sends the message if you choose to send it.

If you email us, we will receive the address and contents you send and may use them to respond and improve the app. Do not include inventory details you do not want to share in a support message.

Voice features — how dictation and Siri actually work

The app uses Apple's built-in speech technologies, and we want to be precise about what that means:

  • Dictation (speech-to-text): When you dictate an entry or a question, the app asks Apple's Speech framework to use on-device recognition. If on-device recognition is unavailable for your device or language, or the on-device request cannot start, the app falls back to Apple's standard Speech recognizer. Depending on your device, language, and settings, Apple may process that fallback recognition request on Apple's servers to produce the transcript; that processing is performed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy and your Siri & Dictation settings. Your audio is never sent to us — we have no server to send it to — and the app receives only the resulting text, which stays on your device.
  • Spoken answers (text-to-speech): Answers are spoken using Apple's on-device speech synthesis. The text of an answer never leaves your device.
  • Siri: When you ask Siri about your inventory (“Where's my passport?”), Siri hands the request to the app on your device; the app looks up the answer in its on-device database and replies. Your inventory is not sent to us. Siri's own handling of your voice request is governed by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Permissions: The app asks for microphone and speech-recognition permission only when you first use a voice feature, and for photo access only when you attach a photo. You can decline or revoke any of these in iOS Settings; typed entry always works without them.

No export — and no copy on our side

The app does not offer a data export feature, on any tier. Your inventory stays in the app on your device. Like other app data, it rides along in your own iCloud or computer (Finder/iTunes) device backups if you use them — those backups are yours, held by Apple or on your computer. We receive no copy and have no access to them.

Deleting your data

Delete any item in the app at any time. Deleting the app from your iPhone deletes all of its data — inventory, photos, settings — completely, because all of it lives only on the device. There is no server copy to delete, and no need to contact us to request deletion.

Children

The app is a general-audience household utility, is not directed at children, and — because it collects no data from anyone — collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and note the new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
