FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. There's nothing to sign up for and nothing to log into. Download it and say where you put something.

Where does my data live?

On your phone. Items, locations, and photos are stored on-device. We have no server and receive nothing — no analytics, no tracking, nothing.

Is my voice recorded or sent anywhere?

Dictation uses Apple's speech recognition, requesting on-device recognition first. PutVoice receives the text of what you said, on your phone. See the privacy policy for the full detail, including Apple's fallback recognition.

Can I export my data?

Honestly: there's no export feature. Your inventory lives on your phone and rides along in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud or Finder), so a phone upgrade carries it over — but if you delete the app without a device backup, the data goes with it. You can share an itemized value list from the app for the insurance use case. Backup and sync are what Plus adds when it launches.

What does it cost?

The app is free, with no item limit. Paid tiers (Plus and Family) arrive later for backup, sync, and sharing — and they'll never gate the basic loop: logging and asking stay free.

Which phones?

PutVoice is an iPhone app.

I lent something out. Does it track that?

Yes. Log who has it and when it's due back; ask where it is and the answer can be “Ben has it, since October 3rd.”
